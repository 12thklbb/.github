# The 12th Kuala Lumpur Boys’ Brigade in Malaysia

This GitHub organisation is for the purpose of hosting relevant code for our website, and any tools that would benefit from GitHub’s features. This is intended for use within our company only.

## About Us

The 12th Kuala Lumpur Boys’ Brigade Company was founded in 2006, and has since grown strongly, rooted in the principles of the Boys’ Brigade. Highly committed to producing world class leaders for the future generation, the company provides training in the form of camps, drill, sports, leadership opportunities and other beneficial activities. We stay true to the BB objective: “The advancement of Christ’s Kingdom among young people and the promotion of habits of Obedience, Reverence, Discipline, Self-respect and all that tends towards a true Christian Character”, and we will always put building the Christian Character as our priority.

Visit [our website](https://bb.isaacaxolotl.com/) to learn more.
